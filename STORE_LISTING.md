## 🇬🇧 English

**Short:** Translate buyer messages and reply in their language in seconds. For Etsy, eBay, Vinted & Depop sellers. No API key.

**Long:**

Babelbee helps online sellers answer every buyer, in every language, in seconds.

Select a buyer's message on any website, right-click "Reply with Babelbee", and get:
• A translation of the message into your language
• A friendly reply written in the buyer's language, based on your shop's real policies
• A back-translation, so you always know exactly what you're sending

WHY SELLERS USE IT
• Works everywhere you sell: Etsy, eBay, Vinted, Depop, Amazon, Shopify inbox, email
• No API key and no setup: uses the AI built into your browser
• Knows your shop: processing times, shipping, returns. It never invents promises you didn't make
• Ready-made templates: where is my order, shipping delays, custom orders, returns, damaged items, offers, review requests
• Private by design: your messages stay in your browser. No servers, no tracking
• Safe: no bots, no auto-sending, no automation. You review and send every message yourself

PLANS
Free: 15 replies per month, forever. Pro: unlimited replies and templates.

Babelbee is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Etsy, eBay, Vinted, Depop or Amazon.

**Search terms / keywords (Edge permite até 7):** etsy seller, translate messages, reply buyers, vinted, ebay seller, customer service, ai reply
