## 7. FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. The free plan gives you 15 replies every month, forever. Upgrade only if you need more.

**Do I need an OpenAI or Gemini account?**
No. Babelbee uses the AI built into recent versions of Chrome. If your computer doesn't support it, you can optionally paste a free Google AI key — we'll show you how in 1 minute.

**Will this get my shop banned?**
Babelbee doesn't automate anything: it never clicks, sends, follows or relists. It only writes text that you review and send yourself.

**Which languages?**
All major languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Japanese and more.

**Does it work with Etsy's own AI assistant?**
Yes, side by side. Etsy's assistant is limited to Etsy (and currently to some US sellers). Babelbee works on every marketplace and in every language.

**Where is my data stored?**
In your browser. Your shop profile and templates are saved in Chrome. Messages are processed on your device (or sent directly to Google only if you choose your own key).

**Is Babelbee affiliated with Etsy, eBay or Vinted?**
No. Babelbee is an independent tool. All trademarks belong to their owners.
