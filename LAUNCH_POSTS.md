## Reddit — r/EtsySellers / r/Etsy

**Title:** How do you handle buyer messages in languages you don't speak?

Body:
> Sellers who ship abroad keep telling me the messages in other languages are the ones they dread. Copy into Google Translate, write a reply, translate back, hope it doesn't sound weird…
>
> I'm building a small Chrome tool for this: select the buyer's message → it shows the translation + a reply in their language based on the shop's policies, plus a back-translation so you know what you're actually sending. It never sends anything automatically.
>
> Before I go further:
> 1. Is this a real problem for you, or is Google Translate good enough?
> 2. What messages do you get most often?
> 3. Would you use Etsy's built-in AI writing assistant for this if you had it?
>
> Happy to share it with anyone who wants to test it.
