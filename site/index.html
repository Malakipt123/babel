Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 15 replies every month, forever. Upgrade only if you need more.

Do I need an OpenAI or Gemini account?

No. Babelbee uses the AI built into recent browsers. If your computer doesn't support it, you can optionally paste a free Google AI key — we show you how in one minute.

Will this get my shop banned?

Babelbee doesn't automate anything: it never clicks, sends, follows or relists. It only writes text that you review and send yourself.

Which browsers?

Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome on desktop. Firefox is on the roadmap.

Does it work alongside Etsy's own AI assistant?

Yes. Etsy's assistant only works inside Etsy. Babelbee works on every marketplace and in every language.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser. Your shop profile and templates are saved locally. Messages are processed on your device, or sent directly to Google only if you choose to use your own key.

Is Babelbee affiliated with Etsy, eBay or Vinted?

No. Babelbee is an independent tool. All trademarks belong to their owners.
