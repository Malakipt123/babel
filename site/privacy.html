Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Babelbee works inside your browser. We don't run servers that receive your messages, we don't track you, and we never sell data.

What is stored

  • Your shop profile, templates and settings — saved in your browser's extension storage.
  • Your usage counter for the free plan — saved locally.
  • Your optional Google AI key — saved locally, never shared with us.

What is sent, and where

  • Built-in browser AI: messages are processed on your device.
  • Your own Google AI key (optional): the message and your shop profile are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API, under Google's terms.
  • License checks (Pro): your license key is sent to our payment provider, Polar, to verify it.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, your email and answers are stored by our form provider (Tally) and used only to contact you about Babelbee. Ask us anytime to delete them.
