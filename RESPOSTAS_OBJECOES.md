# Respostas prontas aos comentários mais prováveis

Regras: ser simpático, curto e honesto. Nunca discutir. Agradecer sempre as críticas: são dados de validação.
Respostas em inglês; adaptar à língua do comentário (o próprio Babelbee servirá para isso 😉).

---

**"Is this spam / self-promo?"**
> Fair question! I'm not selling anything yet — I'm trying to find out if this is a real problem before building more. If it's not useful to you, that's genuinely helpful to know too.

**"Why not just use Google Translate / DeepL?"**
> You totally can! The difference is it's one step instead of four: it translates the buyer's message, writes the reply in their language using your shop's policies (shipping times, returns), and shows you a back-translation so you know exactly what you're sending.

**"Why not ChatGPT?"**
> ChatGPT works too, but you'd have to copy, paste, explain your shop policies every time, translate and copy back. Babelbee remembers your policies and works with a right-click, and it doesn't need an account or an API key.

**"Etsy already has an AI assistant."**
> True, and it's great if you have it! Right now it's limited to some US sellers and only works inside Etsy. Babelbee is for sellers outside the US and for anyone who also sells on Vinted, eBay, Depop or by email.

**"Is it safe for my account? Will I get banned?"**
> It doesn't automate anything: no auto-sending, no clicking, no bots. It only writes text into the reply box (or the side panel) and you press send yourself. To the marketplace it looks exactly like you typing.

**"What happens to my messages / data?"**
> They stay in your browser. It uses the AI built into Chrome/Edge, so messages are processed on your computer. No servers, no tracking. If you choose to use your own Google AI key, messages go directly from your browser to Google.

**"AI replies sound robotic."**
> Agreed, that's the biggest risk! You can choose a tone (friendly, professional, short), add your own templates, and edit before sending. What would make a reply sound like *you*? That would really help me.

**"How much will it cost?"**
> Free plan with 15 replies a month, forever. Pro is $9/month for unlimited. The first 50 sellers on the waitlist can get lifetime access for $49.

**"Does it work on mobile / the Etsy app?"**
> Not yet: it's a desktop browser extension for Chrome and Edge. How much of your messaging do you do on your phone? If it's most of it, I need to know!

**"Firefox / Safari?"**
> Chrome and Edge first; Firefox is on the list. Thanks for asking, I'm counting requests!

**"I only sell in my own country, I don't need translation."**
> Makes sense! It also works for same-language replies (shipping questions, custom orders, returns) using your saved policies. But if translation isn't a pain for you, that's useful feedback.
